# Loneliness, safety and AI for older adults: evidence review for an AI check-in call service for Indian seniors (as of 2026-11-12)

Method note: about 17 searches/fetches (batched). Search-engine summaries were used for many items; only the Hoang 2022, Mehrabi 2025 and Choi 2026 pages were opened in full. Items marked [U] are unverified or rest on secondary sources. No effect size here is invented; where I did not find one, it is listed under Gaps. Many sources were not opened at primary level, so verify before quoting externally.

## 1. Which interventions reduce loneliness in older adults (reviews, effect sizes)

### Takeaway
The evidence base is large but weak: Hoang et al. 2022 rated overall certainty "very low", with high heterogeneity. Multicomponent and group-based programmes, and (in care homes) animal therapy and videoconferencing, show the largest pooled effects. Technology alone in community settings shows a small, non-significant effect. Telephone befriending is plausible but I found no reliable pooled effect size.

### Cited Findings
- Hoang et al., JAMA Netw Open 2022: 70 studies (8,259 participants), 44 in the loneliness meta-analysis (33 community, 3,535 participants; 11 long-term care, 1,057). Overall quality very low, high risk of bias, high heterogeneity, pooled estimates often rest on very few studies — [PMC9577679](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9577679/)
- Same review, SMD for loneliness (negative = less lonely): multicomponent community -0.67 (95% CI -1.13 to -0.21), LTC -0.53 (-0.86 to -0.20); group counselling -0.80 (-1.96 to 0.36), -0.19 (-0.35 to -0.03) after excluding one study; exercise community -0.15 (-0.44 to 0.15); music therapy (1 study) -0.34 (-0.55 to -0.13); "social interventions" community -0.02 (-0.21 to 0.17); CBT/psychotherapy -0.52 (-1.21 to 0.17); animal therapy community -0.41 (-1.75 to 0.92), LTC -1.05 (-2.93 to 0.84) — [PMC9577679](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9577679/)
- Same review, technology: 9 studies pooled; community -0.19 (-0.51 to 0.14, I2 59%), small and non-significant; LTC -1.40 (-2.37 to -0.44, I2 70%), driven by videoconferencing and resting on 2 studies; attrition in LTC technology studies up to 44%. Possible reporting error: the LTC exercise estimate is identical to the LTC multicomponent estimate — [PMC9577679](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9577679/)
- Network meta-analysis of interventions for community-dwelling older adults (2024): pooled SMD -0.95 (95% CI -1.32 to -0.58), described by authors as strong. Only seen via search snippet — [search result](https://bvbvf1.bib-bvb.de/vufind/PrimoRecord/cdi_proquest_miscellaneous_3154888342) [U]
- Meta-analysis of 35 RCTs on "mechanistic" loneliness interventions (2025): pooled SMD -1.11 (95% CI -2.19 to -0.03), I2 99.57% (extreme heterogeneity); larger effects with group formats, longer/more frequent sessions, and cognitive / acceptance-of-ageing content — [PubMed 40930084](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40930084/)
- Meta-analysis of Chinese older adults (2024): Hedges g 0.937 (95% CI 0.71 to 1.16), 36 studies. Snippet only — [search result](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40930084/) [U]
- A 2026 single-author pooled analysis (SMD -0.52, technology-only not significant) is from a journal I could not verify — [ijip.in](https://ijip.in/?p=51254) [U]; treat as low confidence.
- Remote interventions: Fu, Yan & Meng 2022 meta-analysis concluded remotely delivered interventions can reduce loneliness, moderated by media type, strategy, group format and timing; reviewers note inconsistent results due to few, heterogeneous studies. Effect size not obtained — [Frontiers in Psychology 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.935544)
- Phone visiting: Nelson et al. 2022 mixed-methods systematic review found little research on phone visiting's effect on loneliness and mood; supportive but cautious — [SSHRC evidence brief](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/society-societe/community-communite/ifca-iac/evidence_briefs-donnees_probantes/ksg_evidence_briefs/nelson-eng.aspx)
- Park & Khatri (2026) rapid review of RCTs of telephone-delivered interventions for loneliness/depression in 60+: 11 RCTs met criteria (3 telephone vs non-telephone, 8 comparing telephone interventions). Results not retrieved — [Lancashire repository](https://knowledge.lancashire.ac.uk/id/eprint/59985/)
- Importance of the problem: WHO Commission on Social Connection (30 June 2025) estimates about 100 deaths per hour (more than 871,000 a year) associated with loneliness; 1 in 6 people worldwide affected; about 24% in low-income countries vs 11% in high-income (secondary reports); association, not proven causation — [WHO news item](https://www.who.int/NEWS/ITEM/30-06-2025-SOCIAL-CONNECTION-LINKED-TO-IMPROVED-HEATH-AND-REDUCED-RISK-OF-EARLY-DEATH), [WHO Commission page](https://www.who.int/groups/commission-on-social-connection)
- US Surgeon General advisory (May 2023) frames disconnection as comparable to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day (the advisory's own framing). Nature Human Behaviour meta-analysis (June 2023, more than 2 million adults, 90 studies) found social isolation raised early death risk by 32% and loneliness by 14% — [Healio summary](https://www.healio.com/news/primary-care/20230707/social-isolation-loneliness-increase-risk-for-mortality), [WebMD](https://www.webmd.com/balance/news/20230620/socially-isolated-lonely-people-more-likely-to-die-early)

### Inferences
- Pooled numbers are not reliable enough to promise a specific loneliness reduction; a founder should treat "small to moderate, uncertain" as the honest prior.
- The pattern (group, multicomponent, structured, human-delivered content beat unstructured tech in the community) suggests an AI call should connect people to humans and activities rather than be the end point.
- Pets/animal therapy and social prescribing were not separately quantified beyond Hoang 2022's animal therapy figures; no Cochrane/Campbell result was retrieved.

### Gaps
- No pooled effect size for telephone befriending; Cochrane / Campbell reviews and Campaign to End Loneliness review not retrieved.
- Social prescribing effect sizes and the Lancet Healthy Longevity items not searched.
- 2024 network meta-analysis, Chinese meta-analysis and 2025 mechanistic meta-analysis seen at snippet level only.

## 2. Evidence specifically for AI and robots

### Takeaway
Social-robot meta-analysis shows a sizeable pooled loneliness reduction but is biased toward institutional settings, small samples, and possible publication bias. Vendor ElliQ results (95% reduced loneliness) are self-reported and uncontrolled. The best CareCall evidence concerns who answers, not whether it reduces loneliness or deaths.

### Cited Findings
- Mehrabi & Ghezelbash 2025, The Gerontologist 65(12): 19 studies, 1,083 participants, 42 effect sizes; pooled d = -0.590 (95% CI -0.919 to -0.261). Controlled studies (8): d = -0.878 (CI -1.71 to 0.042, wide); uncontrolled (11): d = -0.403 (-0.639 to -0.167). Larger effects in institutions than in own homes; no moderation by cognitive status, age, robot type, duration. Egger's test suggests publication bias, effect may be overestimated; most studies from high-income countries — [PMC12598933](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12598933/)
- Paro / robopets: Abbott et al. 2019 review (19 studies) found reduced agitation with Paro, but loneliness benefits were not statistically significant across trials; a 2021 review of Paro RCTs said benefit in aged care remained unclear — [NIHR ARC review page](https://arc-swp.nihr.ac.uk/publications/how-do-robopets-impact-the-health-and-wellbeing-of-residents-in-care-homes-a-systematic-review-of-qualitative-and-quantitative-evidence/) (details via search summary [U])
- ElliQ, NYSOFA pilot (announced 1 Aug 2023): more than 800 New Yorkers 65+; state and vendor report 95% said ElliQ reduced loneliness (self-reported, no control group); over 30 interactions per day, 6 days a week, more than 75% related to wellbeing. Vendor and state sourced, full report not opened — [NYSOFA release](https://aging.ny.gov/news/nysofas-rollout-ai-companion-robot-elliq-shows-95-reduction-loneliness), [Robotics 24/7](https://robotics247.com/article/elliq_companion_robot_reduces_loneliness_95_percent_finds_new_york_state_rollout)
- Intuition Robotics survey: loneliness reduced for 95.9% of 173 respondents; vendor-run, self-report — [Intuition Robotics](https://intuitionrobotics.com/post/the-results-are-in---measuring-the-efficacy-of-elliq) (via search summary [U])
- ElliQ RCT registered: NCT03972787, waitlist design (8 weeks), primary outcome UCLA loneliness; I found no published results — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03972787)
- ElliQ small qualitative study, 9 rural older adults (Gerontechnology): helped relieve loneliness, not physical health; date unconfirmed — [Gerontechnology](https://journal.gerontechnology.org/currentIssueContent.aspx?aid=4832)
- CareCall rural Jeongeup pilot (Choi, Khor, Park, Jang, Gerontechnology 2026): 2,990 participants (mean age 77.9, 68% women, 36% living alone), 12-13 weekly calls over 3 months; about 11.2% never answered any call. Never answering was linked to living with others, lower income, more functional limitations; responsiveness was higher with older age, living alone, more depressive symptoms, and lower with smartphone ownership. Authors state the most vulnerable were least likely to engage. No outcome (loneliness/death) measured — [Gerontechnology 4870](https://journal.gerontechnology.org/currentIssueContent.aspx?aid=4870)
- CareCall latent-class study: engagement is heterogeneous; digital literacy tied to greater engagement — [Gerontechnology 4820](https://journal.gerontechnology.org/currentIssueContent.aspx?aid=4820)
- CareCall CHI 2023 (Jo, Epstein, Jung, Kim): focus groups with 14 users plus operator observation; operators gained insight into users; calls felt more like casual conversation; reliability checks and municipal agreement needed; the bot sometimes said inappropriate things — [arXiv 2402.11353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11353v1) (via search summary [U]). CHI 2024 follow-up: long-term memory led users to disclose more health details [U]
- CareCall scale: adopted in 128 Korean local districts by Aug 2024 (Naver data via press); "90% satisfaction" quote is from an official, method unknown [U]; solitary-death-prevention claims are anecdotal/operator-sourced — [Asia Economy](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2024090509175645982)
- Conversational AI general: MIT Media Lab RCT (981 participants, 4 weeks) plus OpenAI log analysis (about 40 million interactions, 4,076 survey respondents), March 2025: heavy daily use correlated with more loneliness, dependence and less socialisation; correlational, no non-chatbot control; critiqued by Ophir et al. — [arXiv 2503.17473](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2503.17473), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/03/24/chatgpt-making-frequent-users-more-lonely-study-openai-mit-media-lab/)

### Inferences
- No independent, controlled, long-duration evidence shows that an AI phone call reduces loneliness or deaths. The honest claim for a pilot is "reaches and engages people, detects problems", not "cures loneliness".
- CareCall's non-response pattern (poorest, most disabled miss calls) argues for escalation to family or a human when calls go unanswered.

### Gaps
- Independent ElliQ RCT results not found. Full NYSOFA report not read. Any independent longer-term ElliQ retention data not found.
- No independent evaluation of CareCall on solitary deaths found.
- Other AI phone-call pilots (US, UK, India) not found in this pass.

## 3. Usage reality: retention, abandonment, predictors

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and small-sample. Abandonment of smart speakers is driven by lack of perceived value, ability beliefs and shared-space problems; sustained use is tied to routines and companionship value. Telephones that call the user remove the device-learning barrier, which matches CareCall's finding that smartphone owners answered less.

### Cited Findings
- Trajkova & Martin-Hammond 2020 ("Alexa is a Toy"): 8 focus groups, 38 older adults in a Life Plan Community; most became non-users due to difficulty finding valuable uses, ability-related beliefs, and shared-space use — [IU ScholarWorks](https://scholarworks.indianapolis.iu.edu/items/10aaf444-3a20-4708-9e07-08064c84b137)
- Kim & Choudhury 2021 (Computers in Human Behavior): 16-week study, 12 participants aged 65-95; benefits shifted from convenience to companionship; experienced users coped with errors — [datalearner entry](https://datalearner.com/academic/journal-papers/0747-5632/volumes-and-issues/236/paper-detail/38982)
- JMIR mHealth 2024: 20 long-term smart speaker users (6+ months) used them for entertainment, information, medication reminders, companionship, emergency calls — [JMIR](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2024/1/e47472/PDF)
- Upadhyay et al. 2023: 10 long-term care residents using Alexa 1+ year; used for routines, mood, cognitive stimulation, socialisation (via search summary [U])
- LTC technology studies attrition up to 44% (Hoang 2022) — [PMC9577679](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9577679/)
- CareCall: about 11% never answered over 3 months in a rural pilot — [Gerontechnology 4870](https://journal.gerontechnology.org/currentIssueContent.aspx?aid=4870)

### Inferences
- Predictors suggested by the literature (not tested head to head): proactive outreach, voice rather than screen, a concrete routine value (medication, news, music), and fixing errors. Family involvement and human-in-the-loop as predictors of retention were not directly evidenced in what I retrieved.

### Gaps
- No ElliQ retention/abandonment rate from an independent source. No dataset of month-6/12 retention for AI call services. Family involvement as a retention predictor not found.

## 4. Risks and ethics

### Takeaway
Regulators in the US states and China now require disclosure, crisis protocols and dependence safeguards for companion-style AI. Litigation has centred on minors and ChatGPT-type products; older-adult-specific harm evidence is thin. Design for honesty, escalation and consent.

### Cited Findings
- California SB 243: signed 13 Oct 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026; requires self-harm detection protocols and crisis referral, periodic "not human" reminders for known minors, private right of action ($1,000 per violation statutory damages) — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/states-of-play/firms-navigating-ai-chatbot-risks-must-consider-regulatory-bumps)
- New York companion chatbot law: effective 5 Nov 2025, applies to systems that retain prior interactions, initiate unprompted emotional questions and sustain personal dialogue — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/states-of-play/firms-navigating-ai-chatbot-risks-must-consider-regulatory-bumps). Note: an AI check-in that remembers and asks about feelings plausibly fits this description [inference].
- China: measures on human-like / anthropomorphic AI interactive services issued by five agencies (one analysis says effective 15 July 2026; titles and dates differ between sources). Providers must assess emotions and dependence and intervene; reminders (draft: at login, every two hours, or on overdependence); easy exit; the rules encourage elderly companionship applications. Exact elderly-specific safeguards not verified — [Science and Technology Daily](https://www.stdaily.com/web/English/2026-07/28/content_554571.html), [William Fry](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/chinas-draft-framework-for-emotionally-interactive-ai-psychological-safety-rules-for-ai-companions/) [U on details]
- Litigation: Character.AI and Google settled five suits over teen deaths/harm (Jan 2026, terms undisclosed); at least 10 known suits against OpenAI and Character Technologies by end 2025; most adult victims involved ChatGPT, not companion apps; state actions (Kentucky, Pennsylvania) in 2026 — [AEI](https://ctse.aei.org/suicides-settlements-and-unresolved-chatbot-issues-a-long-litigation-road-lies-ahead/), [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/05/16/after-deaths-lawsuits-against-ai-companies-test-a-new-strategy) (law-firm and advocacy-adjacent sources, verify dockets)
- Dependence: MIT/OpenAI 2025 study (see section 2) — heavy use correlated with dependence and less socialisation; correlational — [arXiv 2503.17473](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2503.17473)

### Inferences
- Because the service would be proactive and emotionally conversational, treat NY/California-style duties (disclose AI, no impersonating a human, crisis escalation, usage nudges) as a design baseline even though they do not bind in India.
- Cognitive decline consent, family-surveillance privacy, and deception of people with dementia are real concerns but I did not retrieve primary ethics literature on them.

### Gaps
- EU AI Act treatment of emotion-recognition and companion systems not researched. India's DPDP Act implications not researched.
- No primary-literature sources on anthropomorphism/deception in dementia or on family monitoring consent.
- Replika-specific and older-adult-specific harm reports not found.

## 5. Human + AI hybrids

### Takeaway
I found no study comparing automated calls with and without human escalation. The case rests on CareCall's operating model (municipal staff act on flagged calls) and general befriending reviews.

### Cited Findings
- CareCall is deployed with public-health staff who review call outputs; operators said the system helped them understand users (CHI 2023) — [arXiv 2402.11353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11353v1) [U on details]
- Hoang 2022 finds multicomponent and group-based, human-facilitated interventions have the more consistent effects — [PMC9577679](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9577679/)

### Inferences
- A hybrid is better justified by safety (someone must act on an emergency or no-answer) than by proven loneliness superiority.

### Gaps
- No comparative evidence on ASHA / community health worker or HelpAge telephone models; "friendly caller" programme evaluations not retrieved.

## 6. India-specific evidence

### Takeaway
Loneliness is common but estimates vary by definition (13% to 37%). I found no independent evaluation of Elderline, Goodfellows, or Hindi voice-assistant use by older Indians.

### Cited Findings
- LASI Wave 1 (2017-18), 60+: 13.4% frequent loneliness (Srivastava & Srivastava); about 36% feel lonely (Bansod, IIPS, n=31,902); 80+ study (Pengpid et al., Int Psychogeriatrics 2025, n=3,163) links loneliness to food insecurity, perceived discrimination, disability, female sex, lower life satisfaction — [IIPS](https://www.iipsindia.ac.in/node/10664), [Mahidol repository](https://repository.li.mahidol.ac.th/items/86a3b426-d55a-4da7-8d23-36346907fb4d/full). A "37.2% lonely / 38.8% isolated" figure is from a commercial blog [U] — [Ageasy](https://ageasy.co.in/blogs/general-wellness/38percent-of-indian-seniors-are-socially-isolated)
- Elderline 14567: toll-free, 8 AM-8 PM daily, information, guidance, emotional support and field intervention; piloted in Hyderabad by Telangana government and Tata Trusts (Mar 2019-Sep 2020); Kerala reports 1.5 lakh+ users and about 100+ calls a day (Nov 2025); a 2022 PTI report said 84% of early calls were non-serviceable; Centre directed states to dissolve state offices by 31 March 2026, current status unverified — [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/senior-citizens-welfare/national-helpline-for-senior-citizens-elderline-14567?lgn=en), [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/lifestyle/health/2025/11/29/elder-line-helpline-senior-citizens-kerala.html), [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/news/move-to-wind-up-elderlines-state-level-operations-in-kerala-triggers-concern-for-senior-citizens). No outcome evaluation found.
- Goodfellows (launched Mumbai Aug 2022, Tata-backed): young graduates visit seniors; company reported 20 fellows after 800+ applicants; reported Rs 5,000/month price in one outlet; only press and company statements — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/providing-companionship-to-the-elderly-tata-backed-goodfellows-launches-in-mumbai/1084729). No independent evaluation.
- Digital use: qualitative study of 19 urban Indian adults 55+ (English/Hindi/Kannada) on a WhatsApp-based self-help platform; participants wanted credible, simple content (Springer 2025); a secondary blog claims 17 of 19 used WhatsApp daily [U] — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44192-025-00277-4)

### Inferences
- Because 2G/feature-phone and WhatsApp-voice use are plausible for the target, a voice call (not an app) fits, but this fit is not proven by any study retrieved.

### Gaps
- No study of voice assistants in Hindi/regional languages among older Indians; no COVID-era tele-check-in evaluation retrieved; no data on stigma about monitoring, joint family expectations, or tone (respect forms such as "aap") from primary research.

## 7. Credible measures for a small pilot

### Takeaway
Use validated, brief measures and operational metrics; do not claim causal effects without a comparison group.

### Cited Findings
- The ElliQ trial (NCT03972787) uses the UCLA Loneliness scale as primary outcome with a waitlist control — [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03972787)
- CareCall research used call answer rate / missed-call categories and depressive symptoms as engagement outcomes — [Gerontechnology 4870](https://journal.gerontechnology.org/currentIssueContent.aspx?aid=4870)

### Inferences
- Suggested set (author's judgement, not from a source): UCLA-3 at baseline and 6-8 weeks (ideally with a waitlist group); check-in answer rate and no-answer-to-resolution time; days with human contact per week (self-report); number and time-to-response of escalations; retention at weeks 4, 8, 12; dropout reasons; family or caller satisfaction; adverse events log.

### Gaps
- No normative UCLA-3 data for Indian elders retrieved; sample size needed for detecting an effect not computed.
