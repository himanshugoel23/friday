## Players: what they sell, price, funding, traction

### Takeaway
Funded India elder-care is dominated by human-heavy models (Emoha/Age Care Labs, Portea, Samarth, senior living operators) priced at roughly Rs 1,600-8,000+/month for membership and far more for hands-on care. Only Age Care Labs, Kites, Athulya and Dozee have raised meaningful money; AI-voice seniors products found are tiny and unfunded as far as I can confirm. One shutdown found (GenWise).

## Government programmes and policy

### Takeaway
Policy support is real but benefits-based (health cover, helpline); government procurement of elder AI was not found. NITI Aayog explicitly favours technology and AI for senior care.

### Cited Findings
- Ayushman Vay Vandana: all citizens 70+ eligible regardless of income, Aadhaar e-KYC, free enrolment via Ayushman app / beneficiary.nha.gov.in / CSCs; Rs 5 lakh cover (Business Today 2025). Numbers conflict: over 96 lakh seniors enrolled (Deccan Herald, Feb 2026) vs over 1.14 crore cards issued as of 28 Feb 2026 (Rajya Sabha reply cited by aggregator); eligible pool about 6 crore — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/dhie/news-and-trends/2026/02/03/over-96-lakh-seniors-enrolled-in-ayushman-vay-vandana-scheme); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/insurance/story/govt-rolls-out-ayushman-bharat-cards-for-70-seniors-with-rs-5-lakh-free-treatment-heres-how-to-apply-477945-2025-05-27)
- NITI Aayog "Senior Care Reforms in India" position paper (Feb 2024): national senior care portal, tax/GST changes, savings and reverse mortgage, CSR funds for senior care, emphasis on tech and AI — [BioSpectrum](https://www.biospectrumindia.com/public/news/22/24228/niti-aayog-releases-report-for-senior-care-reforms-in-india.html); [Eastern Mirror](https://www.easternmirrornagaland.com:443/niti-aayog-calls-for-prioritising-tech-ai-for-senior-care-in-india)

## Regulation for an AI + human elder service (not legal advice)

### Takeaway
Biggest constraints: DPDP (most duties start about May 2027), TRAI rules on automated calls (consent, DLT, in flux), WhatsApp bot scope limit, and medical-device status if hardware/software makes health claims. Counsel needed on all.

### Cited Findings
- DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13-14 Nov 2025, phased over 12-18 months. Rules 1, 2, 17-21 immediate; Rules 3, 5-16, 22, 23 (notices, consent, breach, children's data) after 18 months, about 13-14 May 2027; Consent Manager (Rule 4) timing conflicts between sources (one year vs later notification) — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/11/14/del148-biz-dpdp-rules-ld-govt.html); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/). I did not read the gazette text.
- Consent on behalf of others: repo note says guardian consent rule (Rule 11) covers persons with disabilities and children, not ordinary adult seniors, so an adult senior must consent personally — [dpdpa.com Rule 11](https://www.dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule11.html) [U, not re-verified here]. Implication: the paying child cannot consent for a competent parent; flag for counsel.
- TRAI: TCCCPR amended Feb 2025 (explicit consent for auto-dialler/robo calls); promotional calls on DLT, designated number series; a TRAI direction on AI/ML for unsolicited commercial communication reported 27 Feb 2026 (Khaitan note, 12 Mar 2026); a draft Third Amendment (2026) referencing robo and artificial voice; AI-voice disclosure and penalties figures (e.g. Rs 10 lakh) come from a vendor blog and are [U]. AI voice calls to a consenting customer for service are probably not "promotional", but classification needs counsel — [Khaitan ERGO](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-03/ERGO%20-%20TRAI%20Direction%20on%20AI%20&%20ML%20for%20UCC%20-%2012%20March%202026.pdf); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/law-firms/view-point/trais-crackdown-on-spam-calls-and-ai-driven-telemarketing)
- WhatsApp Business: from 15 Jan 2026 Meta bars general-purpose AI chatbots (LLM assistants) from the Business Solution; task-specific bots (support, notifications) remain allowed. Global policy; India-specific framing is one vendor [U] — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-bans-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp-business-api-chatgpt-to-shut-down-jan-2026-125102200335_1.html); [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)
- CDSCO Guidance Document on Medical Device Software (CDSCO/MD/GD/MDSW/01/2026, finalised about mid-2026) under MDR 2017: software is a device only if intended for a medical purpose; classes A-D; intended use is the test. No source places fall detection in a class; an alert-only fall wearable's status needs check against the First Schedule and the guidance examples — [CDSCO guidance PDF](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/Guidance-document-on-Medical-Device-Software-under-MDR-2017.pdf); [Cyril Amarchand blog](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/01/medical-device-as-software-has-cdsco-guidance-changed-the-rules/)
- Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020: only a registered medical practitioner may teleconsult; AI cannot counsel or prescribe (repo note citing Mondaq/Khaitan) — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/healthcare/921874/telemedicine-practice-guidelines) [U not re-verified]
- E-pharmacy: draft 2018 rules never notified as of mid-2026; no statutory definition; sale needs Drugs and Cosmetics licence; Times of India Feb 2026 report of AI-fake prescriptions accepted [U secondary] — [LSHTM](https://www.lshtm.ac.uk/media/102426); [Medical Dialogues](https://business.medicaldialogues.in/health-ministry-draft-rules-on-sale-of-drugs-by-e-pharmacy-details)
- RBI: BBPS Master Direction 29 Feb 2024 regulates bill payment collection; paying on someone's behalf from own funds unresolved (repo note) — [RBI](https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/87730/39710850/MD+on+BBPS+-+29+02+2024+-+Final.pdf) [U]. RBI April 2026 discussion paper floated trusted-contact approval for 70+ high-value UPI; proposal only (repo note).
- CNAP caller-name display directed from 1 Nov 2026 (repo note; rollout status unconfirmed).

### Inferences
- A WhatsApp assistant limited to elder-care tasks fits the "task-specific" carve-out; a generic Q&A assistant would not.
- A SOS/fall wearable that only alerts contacts is likely lower-risk than one that diagnoses, but a classification opinion is needed before making any health claim.

### Gaps
- Not researched or verified: BIS/WPC certification specifics for wearables, misleading health claims (CCPA, Drugs and Magic Remedies Act, ASCI), consumer protection for subscriptions, Digital Arrest/IT Rules on AI labelling, DLT registration steps for a service-call use case, insurance regulation (IRDAI).
- Primary texts (DPDP gazette, TCCCPR consolidated, CDSCO guidance PDF) were not read in full.

## Gaps in the market (needs no Indian player serves well)

### Takeaway
Evidence suggests open space in scam protection with voice, voice-first non-English AI check-ins, Tier-2/3 reach, and low price points; but this is inference from absence of evidence, not a verified negative.

### Cited Findings
- Incumbent membership prices published are Rs 6,399+/month (Emoha plan page), with care-manager fees Rs 8,000-15,000/month (Samarth) — see above; no sub-Rs 1,000 funded offering found other than an older Emoha Rs 1,599 listing [U].
- Scam context: repo note records digital-arrest and KYC scams against seniors, free Truecaller Family Protection (March 2026) and Google call-time protections — repo note PARENT_CARE_RESEARCH.md [U not re-verified].
- AI voice-first senior products exist (Saathi AI, JunoDost) but only as founder-posted early listings with no traction data.

### Inferences
- Gap 1: scam protection plus family alerts bundled with human fallback, delivered by phone call.
- Gap 2: Indian-language voice for non-English seniors with escalation to humans; the early AI entrants are unfunded and unproven.
- Gap 3: Tier-2/3 cities: Emoha claims 120 cities [U] but human responders concentrate in metros; physical services thin elsewhere.
- Gap 4: under Rs 1,000/month: unserved by funded players, but unit-economics are hard (Emoha loses Rs 0.5 on each rupee earned per Entrackr; repo note says spends Rs 1.5 per Rs 1 earned).

### Gaps
- No user research, complaint data, or willingness-to-pay data from this round.
