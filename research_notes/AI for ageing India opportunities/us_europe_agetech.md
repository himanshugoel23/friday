# AgeTech in the USA and Europe: products, business models, outcomes (as of 2026-11-12)

Method note: ~22 tool calls (web search snippets plus 5 page fetches). Items marked [U] come from search-result summaries or secondary/aggregator pages that I did not open at the primary source, or could not verify. Vendor claims are labelled "vendor". Many requested items (Life Alert, Aloe Care, Apple Watch, Carefull bank partners, Wellthy/Cariloop/Carallel, Care.com, Nordic welfare tech, pill dispensers, hardware BOM) were NOT researched to a citable level; see Gaps.

## Companion and voice AI: ElliQ, Alexa Together, GrandPad, AI phone companions

### Takeaway
ElliQ is the one companion product with a real government-payer channel (NYSOFA, free to the user), but its headline "94-95% less lonely" numbers are vendor/programme self-reports, not controlled evidence. Amazon's paid caregiver service Alexa Together launched in Dec 2021 and was shut down in May 2025, a sign that consumer-paid remote-caregiving subscriptions on a general voice platform did not hold.

### Cited Findings
- NYSOFA signed a new three-year, $2.1M agreement with Intuition Robotics (article dated 2026-10-13). "Hundreds" of older adults have received ElliQ since 2022; 3,500+ applied in 2025. Unit count, per-unit cost and funding source for the $2.1M are not stated. — [The Robot Report, Oct 2026](https://www.therobotreport.com/nysofa-expands-partnership-intuition-robotics-bring-elliq-more-seniors/)
- 2026 programme data as reported there (programme/vendor-reported, self-selected users, no control group): 94% report feeling less lonely, 97% improved wellbeing, 92% find it useful, 79% feel more connected, average 48 interactions per day (up from 29). — [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/nysofa-expands-partnership-intuition-robotics-bring-elliq-more-seniors/)
- Earlier NYSOFA pilot headline was 95% reduction in loneliness among 800+ participants (NYSOFA and Intuition Robotics). — [Robotics 24/7](https://robotics247.com/article/elliq_companion_robot_reduces_loneliness_95_percent_finds_new_york_state_rollout) [U, snippet only]
- Eligibility reportedly: age 75+, internet access, English-speaking, spends much time alone; units recycled when a participant dies or enters care. — [AOL/Fortune-style syndication](https://www.aol.com/articles/ai-robots-moving-lonely-elderly-115800155.html) [U]
- One critical analysis (secondary blog) says the 95% figure comes from a pilot not a trial, in a self-selected screened group, and measures willingness to report less loneliness. — [tilacum](https://tilacum.vercel.app/news/ai-companions-elder-loneliness-2026) [U, low-quality source; the peer-reviewed ElliQ studies it cites were not retrieved]
- ElliQ consumer pricing (vendor FAQ): one-time enrollment fee $249.99 plus a monthly subscription; third parties cite about $59-60/month; older TechCrunch coverage said $30/month on annual plan. — [ElliQ FAQ](https://elliq.com/pages/faqs); [Senior List](https://www.theseniorlist.com/aging-in-place/elliq) [U on current monthly price]
- Intuition Robotics funding: $36M Series B (Feb 2020); $25M in 2023 of which $20M venture capital and the rest venture debt. A $83M "total" on one profile is unverified and conflicts with other listed rounds. — [TechCrunch Aug 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/29/eldercare-robot-elliq-nabs-another-25-million-in-funding/); [mobihealthnews](https://mobihealthnews.com/node/153372); [startupim](https://startupim.com/company/intuition-robotics) [U]
- Alexa Together launched Dec 2021 at $19.99/month or $199/year (six-month free trial) with 24/7 Urgent Response, activity feed, Remote Assist, and third-party fall detection (Vayyar Care, SkyAngelCare). Amazon's page carries a note dated 2025-05-21: no longer available; customers pointed to Alexa Emergency Assist. Amazon gave no reason in what I retrieved. — [Amazon launch page](https://www.aboutamazon.com/md/news/devices/alexa-together-launches-to-help-customers-remotely-care-for-loved-ones)
- GrandPad: subscription bundling tablet, support, 4G LTE, insurance; month-to-month (vendor). An aggregator lists ~$18.7M raised, unverified. No price or revenue found. — [GrandPad press](https://grandpad.net/press-releases/grandie-games-surpasses-10-000-downloads-strengthening-family-bonds-through-play); [employbl](https://employbl.com/companies/grandPad/funding-rounds) [U]
- Investor view (April 2025 MedCity panel): Care.Coach (AI companion) backer says barrier to entry is low because developers build on OpenAI-type platforms, so moats are hard; another investor says AI companions work best alongside human connection. — [MedCity News, Apr 2025](https://medcitynews.com/2025/04/agetech-venture-capital-healthcare-medicare/)

### Inferences
- The only validated payer for a companion device in the evidence found is a state agency (NYSOFA); contract size ($2.1M over three years) is small relative to the $25-36M the company raised, implying government contracts alone do not fund a hardware company [inference].
- Amazon exiting Alexa Together suggests a $20/month remote-caregiver add-on on a mass voice platform lacked enough paying demand [inference; Amazon gave no reason].

### Gaps
- No independent peer-reviewed ElliQ loneliness data retrieved; no churn data for ElliQ, GrandPad or AI check-in call startups; no AI phone-companion startup pricing or traction found (not searched beyond Care.Coach mention).
- ElliQ per-unit cost to NYSOFA, and the funding source of the contract, not found.

## PERS and fall detection

### Takeaway
Evidence retrieved is thin: PERS is a roughly mature, consolidating, small-market category; false alarms and non-wearing are recognised problems, but I found no quantified abandonment study.

### Cited Findings
- Best Buy agreed in 2018 to buy GreatCall (Lively) for $800M cash; Connect America acquired Philips Lifeline's North American business (and startup 100Plus). — [ABI Research](https://www.abiresearch.com/market-research/insight/7779778-connect-americas-summer-acquisitions-refle?hsLang=en) [U on exact dates, snippet]
- WaterStreet invested $100M in Medical Guardian in January 2021 (trade blog). — [Age in Place Tech](https://ageinplacetech.com/blog/leave-no-trend-behind-yet-another-pers-smartwatch-ticks) [U]
- A trade blog puts PERS at a "$1-2 billion market" versus remote patient monitoring at "$16 billion" (opinion, not a market study). — same source [U]
- Trade article: false alarms worry users and caregivers and sometimes cause the older adult not to wear the device. Patent filings state that wrist-worn fall detection accuracy is particularly low and that sensitivity and false alarms trade off. — [McKnight's Senior Living](https://www.mcknightsseniorliving.com/?p=8037) [U]

### Inferences
- Apple Watch and smartphones (Apple Watch fall detection since 2018, not verified here) squeeze pendant PERS; incumbents respond by selling to health plans and adding RPM [inference].

### Gaps
- Life Alert, Aloe Care, Apple Watch fall detection specifics; PERS margins, pricing ranges, abandonment rates (e.g. peer-reviewed mPERS non-use studies), false-alarm rates: not found.

## Passive monitoring and sensors

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced buyer is the home care agency or facility, not the family. Sensi.ai (audio monitoring sold to agencies) is raising steadily; SafelyYou (facilities) closed a Series C.

### Cited Findings
- Sensi.AI: $31M Series B (June 2024, Insight Partners, Zeev) and $45M Series C (Oct 2025, led by Qumra Capital); company claims 400% revenue growth in the past year and ~80% of the largest home care networks as customers (vendor claims). Total raised reported as $93.7M-$98M+ (sources disagree). Named customers include Right at Home, Visiting Angels, Comfort Keepers. — [Newswire Series C](https://www.newswire.com/news/sensi-ai-raises-45m-series-c-extending-its-dominance-of-care-22653210); [Insight Partners](https://www.insightpartners.com/?p=20733)
- SafelyYou: $43M Series C led by Touring Capital, total raised $100M+ (dated about Jan 2025). — [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/safelyyou-secures-43m-ai-assisted-senior-living-and-more-funding-news?leadId=1491391) [U on date]
- CarePredict: last found round $29M Series A-3 in 2023. — [Age in Place Tech/MobiHealthNews listing](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/inspiren-raises-70m-ai-senior-care-platform) [U]
- Inspiren raised $70M for an AI senior care platform (date not verified). — same link [U]

### Inferences
- Funding is flowing to products that sell to provider organisations (B2B) and raise caregiver productivity [inference].

### Gaps
- Vayyar, Envoy at Home: nothing found. No independent outcome data (falls reduced, cost savings) retrieved for any sensor vendor.

## Human-service marketplaces: Papa, Honor/Home Instead, Care.com

### Takeaway
Papa grew by selling a supplemental benefit to Medicare Advantage plans, then drew Senate scrutiny over safety. The payer model is exposed to MA benefit cuts. Honor bought Home Instead (2021) and restructured by 2023.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 KFF Health News investigation: Medicare plans pay Papa, a for-profit, a per-member monthly fee for "Pals" doing companionship, errands, rides. — [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/medicare-advantage-plans-senior-companions-profits/)
- Senate Aging Committee (Chairman Casey) letter to CMS, 2024-03-27: allegations of harassment, assault, theft and unsafe conditions reported by Bloomberg; Papa logged 1,200+ confidential complaints in four years (per the letter); partnered with 65+ health plans; operates in all 50 states; "tens of millions" in annual revenue. Casey asked how CMS vets such supplemental benefits. — [Senate Aging Committee press release](https://www.aging.senate.gov/press-releases/casey-urges-medicare-agency-to-prevent-abuse-of-older-adults-and-workers-at-caregiving-tech-company)
- Papa (vendor, Aug 2026) announced an expanded multi-year MA partnership with EternalHealth in Massachusetts and Arizona; its 2025 partner summit recap is upbeat about SSBCI as the way forward. — [HIT Consultant](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/08/18/papa-expands-multi-year-medicare-advantage-partnership-with-eternalhealth-across-massachusetts-and-arizona/); [Papa blog](https://papa-site.production.papa.com/resources/blog/4-takeaways-from-papas-2025-partner-summit)
- Major MA carriers are trimming supplemental benefits and exiting markets (Becker's, 2025-26). — [Becker's Payer](https://www.beckerspayer.com/payer/medicare-advantage/insurers-pull-medicare-advantage-plans-as-profit-pressures-mount/)
- Investor Zamkow (Third Act Ventures): a wave of supplemental-benefit plans was undone by star-rating changes; models can vanish within a couple of years. — [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2025/04/agetech-venture-capital-healthcare-medicare/)
- Honor acquired Home Instead on 2021-08-06; 15% of HQ staff laid off in a June 2023 restructure; new CFO and CLO named Sept 2025; Home Instead delivers 60M+ hours of care annually (company). — [Aging Here, Jun 2023](https://aginghere.beehiiv.com/p/jun-30-2023-aginghere-evolving-home-care-honors-restructure-techdriven-approach); [Senior Housing News](https://seniorhousingnews.com/2021/08/06/honor-acquires-home-instead-in-home-care-deal-with-senior-living-implications/)

### Gaps
- Papa funding totals, valuation, any 2024-2026 layoffs or outcomes of CMS response; Care.com economics; Honor revenue/profit: not found.

### Cited Findings

## Europe: UK telecare, Nordic welfare tech, Tunstall, Birdie, Cera, Nobi

### Takeaway
UK telecare is paid mostly by local authorities and housing providers, and the analogue-to-digital switchover is an unfunded pressure. Cera raised large sums as a digital-first home care provider. Nordic and Nobi evidence not found.

### Cited Findings
- UK government announced a Telecare National Action Plan (Feb 2025); Tunstall says it no longer makes analogue-only telecare devices in the UK; local authorities are funding the transition from limited budgets; Tunstall (vendor, Jan 2025) recommends targeted NHS England funding via ICSs and cites Spain and Australia as having central funding. — [Tunstall blog Feb 2025](https://www.tunstall.com/news--events/blog/2025/02/the-uk-government-announce-telecare-national-action-plan/); [Tunstall short report Jan 2025](https://www.tunstall.com/globalassets/shortreport/advancing-technology-enabled-care---a-short-report-january-2025.pdf)
- BT/Openreach copper switch-off delayed from end-December 2025 to 2027-01-31; a 2024 figure via Housing LIN says an average of 20% of calls from analogue telecare equipment fail to connect on digital lines. — [Tunstall blog Jan 2026](https://www.tunstall.co.uk/news-events/blog/2026/01/the-analogue-to-digital-switchover-housing-cannot-afford-to-drift/); [Housing LIN](https://www.housinglin.org.uk/blogs/TAPPI-Days-A-Telecare-National-Plan-for-Action/)
- Cera: January 2025 raise of about $150M (£123M+), debt and equity, led by BDT & MSD Partners and Schroders Capital (one report says SoftBank Vision Fund 2 led an earlier $150M Series B; sources conflict); company says about 10,000 caregivers and nurses. Cera is a digital-first care provider, not pure software. — [Digital Health](https://www.digitalhealth.net/2025/01/cera-secures-more-than-123m-to-roll-out-ai-led-home-healthcare); [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/uk-healthcare-tech-leader-cera-secures-150m-to-expand-ai-driven-home-care-services-2025-01-14)
- Birdie (home care software, UK): $30M Series B reported, 1,000+ providers (date and current status not verified). — [SignalBase](https://www.trysignalbase.com/news/funding/birdie-raises-300) [U]
- UK AgeTech venture funding of GBP 14.6M in 2025 vs GBP 6.8M in 2024. — [Beauhurst](https://www.beauhurst.com/blog/uks-top-agetech-startups/) [U, snippet]

### Gaps
- Nobi, Nordic welfare tech reimbursement, Birdie's fate (the 2025-26 status), Tunstall ownership/financials: not found.

## Funding, outcomes and investor views; YC request

### Takeaway
Investors in April 2025 said AgeTech lacks proven business models and "big, visible wins"; capital concentrated in B2B (agencies, facilities, providers).

### Cited Findings
- Garber (7wireVentures): doubts most direct-to-consumer AgeTech has staying power, most shifted to B2B, wants "hard dollar ROIs," expects government funding to shrink. MedCity panel concluded the sector lacks dedicated funding, proven models and big exits. — [MedCity News, Apr 2025](https://medcitynews.com/2025/04/agetech-venture-capital-healthcare-medicare/)
- Equitage Ventures closed $47.3M for senior care investing (Apr 2025). — [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/equitage-ventures-secures-47-3m-to-expand-investments-in-senior-care-2025-04-23) [U, snippet]
- YC Requests for Startups, Fall 2026: "AI for the Aging Population," author Max Kolysh. Reported content: demographic premise (1 in 5 Americans over 65 by 2030) and not enough caregivers; asks for a production-ready platform for seniors 65+ and families with voice-first companion, gentle safety/wellness monitoring, shared caregiver dashboard, consent and dignity. Exact original wording NOT obtained; I could only see a third-party mirror, and the live YC page (which now lists Winter 2027) did not include it. Short quoted fragment from mirror: "The population is aging and there aren't enough caregivers." — [Modelence mirror](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-fall-2026/ai-for-the-aging-population); [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)

## Hardware vs software economics; payer lessons

### Takeaway
No cited unit-economics data (BOM, certification, returns, support) was found. Only indirect evidence is available.

### Cited Findings
- ElliQ's model pairs a ~$250 enrollment fee with a recurring subscription, and NYSOFA pays on behalf of users (see above). — [ElliQ FAQ](https://elliq.com/pages/faqs)
- Alexa Together ($20/month) was discontinued, whereas B2B software/monitoring (Sensi.ai) is raising Series C rounds. — sources above.
- Payer pattern: consumer-pay DTC viewed sceptically by investors; Medicare Advantage supplemental benefits volatile (star ratings, benefit cuts); government (NYSOFA, UK local authorities) pays but with small, budget-constrained contracts; banks as a channel for scam protection (EverSafe/Carefull). — MedCity, Becker's, Tunstall, EverSafe sources above.

### Inferences
- For a country without Medicare Advantage or state-funded devices, the transferable lessons are: sell to organisations whose labour costs the product reduces (agencies, facilities, banks) or to families via a trusted distributor, and avoid depending on a single reimbursement benefit [inference from the above, not from a source].

### Gaps
- BOM, certification (FCC/FDA/UL), returns, support burden, small hardware teams that died: not researched. Needs dedicated searches (e.g. Gerontechnologist, Lively/ Aloe Care case studies).
