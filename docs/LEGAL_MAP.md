# Legal map for consumer calls (D10)

Status: ALL ROWS ARE FROM GENERAL KNOWLEDGE, UNVERIFIED. Nothing here is legal advice and no row has
been checked by a lawyer. Each row: what the code does today, the exact question for a lawyer, and
whether it blocks a launch with real recipients. "Blocks" = do not call real, non-founder numbers until answered.

| # | Rule area | What we do today | Exact question for a lawyer | Blocks launch? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TRAI telemarketing rules and DLT registration | Calls are placed on behalf of one user to a person they chose (a personal or service call), not promotion. No bulk calling: per-user daily caps, group limit (default 10), per-number caps across users. | Is an AI-assisted call a user makes through Friday to a chosen person a "transactional/service" call outside the commercial-communication rules? Do we need DLT/principal-entity registration, and which header/series must outbound calls use? | Yes |
| 2 | TRAI/DoT rules on automated or AI-voice calls | The assistant states at the start that it is an assistant (fixed line, W2). No cloned voice. | Is there a current rule requiring disclosure, consent or a specific number series for AI/pre-recorded/synthetic voice calls? What exact disclosure wording is required? | Yes |
| 3 | Calling hours | Gate window per callee kind: businesses, customer care, officials, unknown 09:00-19:00 IST; family/friends 08:00-21:30 (settings `FRIDAY_CALLS_WINDOW_*`). | Is there a legal calling-hours limit that applies to non-commercial calls? Are the windows right for each kind? | No (settings are conservative) |
| 4 | Caller ID / CLI | One caller number from the pool, consistent, never hidden or spoofed. | Which number series must be used for outbound calls from an app, must the number be one the caller owns, and what rules apply to CLI display and spoof prevention? | Yes |
| 5 | Call recording consent | Recording is stored, retention 30 days, user can delete now; NOT announced on the call (founder decision, 19 Oct). | Is recording a call where one party (the user's assistant) records without telling the other side lawful in India, and is the store-but-do-not-announce choice acceptable? What notice is required if not? | Yes |
| 6 | DPDP Act 2023 for non-user recipients | Recipient number stored encrypted only for the user's own request; do-not-call keeps only a keyed hash; contacts are per user and not synced; erasure of a user removes their call data. | What is our lawful purpose and notice duty toward a recipient who never signed up? Which retention is allowed for transcripts and recordings of recipients? How do recipients exercise access/erasure (we offer `export` and `delete` on the registry)? | Yes |
| 7 | WhatsApp Business policy | Approval, reports and questions are sent inside the 24-hour window by the channel layer; copy is pending founder approval. | Does a bot that reports a phone call outcome and proposes calls fit WhatsApp Business messaging and AI-provider policy? Are template categories right? | Check before launch |
| 8 | Impersonation and fraud law (IT Act, IPC/BNS) | Friday never claims to be the user, never clones a voice, refuses impersonation, scams, threats, pranks, harassment (deterministic rules, strikes, bans). | Does our refusal set cover our exposure for an assistant used to harass or defraud? Is there a duty to report or retain evidence of abuse? | No |
| 9 | Emergency numbers | Gate blocks 112, 100, 101, 102, 108, 1091, 1098 and other public-safety short codes in all written forms. | Is the list complete and current? Are there duties when a user describes an emergency to us (we route to 112, no call)? | No |
| 10 | Do-not-call / NDNC registry | Global internal registry by hashed number, checked before every dial; opt-out phrases detected on the call. | Must we also scrub against the national preference register for any call type? What is the deadline to honour an opt-out? | Yes |
| 11 | RBI rules (banking, payments) | Friday never handles OTP, PIN, card numbers or payments; scam rules refuse OTP requests; customer-care calls share no credentials. | Does calling a bank's customer care on a customer's behalf, with the customer's approved facts, raise authentication or outsourcing concerns? | No |
| 12 | IRDAI / SEBI | No advice on insurance or investments; calls only ask facts the user approved. | Could relaying a user's request about a policy or investment be "advice" or an intermediary activity? | No |
| 13 | Consumer protection (e-commerce, unfair practice, liability) | Every call needs the user's approval of the exact plan and opening line; commitments not in the plan become "needs your OK"; reports list what was agreed. | Who is liable if the assistant misstates something or a commitment is misunderstood? What terms of service wording is needed? | Yes |
| 14 | Medical, legal, minors, self-harm | Refuse or route (fixed lines): no medical or legal advice, no calls to children, self-harm routes to support. Helpline numbers still to be added after verification. | Which helpline numbers and wording are appropriate? Any duty of care when a user mentions self-harm? | No |
| 15 | Data localisation and cross-border processing | Data in the project database; model and speech providers per W2/lead. | Do call transcripts and recordings processed by foreign providers need extra consent or localisation? | Check before launch |
