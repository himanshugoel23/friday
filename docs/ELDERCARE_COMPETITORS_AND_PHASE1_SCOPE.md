**Pattern [A]:** the three human jobs everybody keeps: (1) a named coordinator who talks to the family on a schedule (Portea, Samarth, Emoha); (2) an emergency call centre or responder (Emoha, Aloe, Lifeline); (3) hands-on carers in the home (all). **What this says about concierge economics [A]:** every pure errand business that tried to scale on volume died or shrank (Exec, Zirtual, Dunzo, Genie, chat concierges). Survivors either charge a high subscription (Quintessentially), cap tasks per month (Fancy Hands, Emoha 6 h), or sit inside another revenue stream (bank cards, Emoha membership). **Robin's included concierge must be capped, like Emoha's 6 hours and Fancy Hands' task bundles, or it will be a cost sink.**

### 3.2 What does NOT scale for 2-3 part-timers and 20-200 families
1. Anything physical: escorts, pharmacy runs, home visits, tech help on site. Cost is hours per task, not minutes, and needs insurance and background checks. Papa's safety record shows the risk [S].
2. A dedicated named care manager per family (Portea, Samarth model): at 20 min/week/family a part-timer tops out near 50-60 families [A], and the buyers pay Rs 8-15k/month for it [S].
3. "24/7 human emergency desk": 5-6 people per seat [ROBIN_CONCIERGE_DESK.md]; Emoha burns 42% of costs on staff and still loses money [S].
4. Unlimited errands. Every unlimited errand business above shut or shrank [S].
5. Anything urgent ("within the hour") outside desk hours; it forces on-call pay for everyone.
6. Cross-city local knowledge (parents in 30 towns): a concierge in Delhi cannot judge a Patna clinic; remote phone work fails [A]. Cap Phase 1 to 2-3 metro areas for parents.
7. Handling documents, OTPs or money: breaks "never pay, never ask OTP" and invites fraud on the staff themselves.

### 3.3 Recommended Phase 1 scope in three tiers

**MUST (safety, already built or in backlog):** daily check-in and retry ladder, medicine reminders and refill alerts, SOS and fall flow, scam check on forwards, missed-call voice agent, unreachable escalation, human emergency lane in stated desk hours, support line (WhatsApp + phone), incident log and weekly review. Needs the pilot-blocker items in ROBIN_PHASE1_BACKLOG.md section 2 (Meta, legal, voice approval).

**SHOULD: nine concierge request types, all with a ticket, a proof and an SLA** (draft, [U] until pilot data; matches the 15-min ack and 4-hour finish draft in ROBIN_CONCIERGE_DESK.md):

| # | Request type | Draft SLA | Proof returned | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Book doctor or lab slot (call or app) | ack 15 min, done same day | confirmation message/photo | booking only, no advice |
| 2 | Book cab for appointment (user pays in app) | 30 min | booking screenshot | ride app, family card |
| 3 | Call a shop/clinic/plumber/electrician and report back | 4 h | call note | script and recording-free note |
| 4 | Pay a bill (UPI link sent, user taps) | 4 h | receipt screenshot | staff never pay, never see PIN |
| 5 | Order groceries on the family's app account | 4 h | order screenshot | family-owned account, payment by family |
| 6 | Reorder regular medicine from a licensed e-pharmacy on the child's account | 4 h; same day | order screenshot | prescription on file, child confirms, no substitution |
| 7 | Find and explain a step-by-step procedure (pension life certificate, gas KYC, bank branch) | next day | 3-line voice note | information only |
| 8 | Reminders and follow-ups for appointments, renewals, refunds | 1 day | confirmation | |
| 9 | Phone-based tech guidance for the parent (not remote control) | 4 h | call note | only on parent's own request |

Cap: fair-use of 2 requests per family per month included at pilot start, then review; Emoha caps at 6 hours/month [S], Fancy Hands bundles tasks [S]. Founder to set.

**LATER:** hospital escort or companion via partner (child pays partner), tele-consult link-out, home nursing/physio referrals, document pick-up, grievance SLA publication, 24/7 desk, per-family named care manager.

### 3.4 Staffing and cost per 100 families
All figures [U], arithmetic on stated assumptions; real pilot data replaces them at 20, 100, 300 families.

| Line | Assumption | Hours/week | Cost/month (Rs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concierge requests | 0.4 requests/family/week x 100 x 20-30 min | 13-20 | |
| Triage, follow-up, proof checks | 50% overhead | 7-10 | |
| Support line + onboarding calls | 10 min/family/month onboarding, plus grievances | 5-8 | |
| Emergency lane 2 | 1 seat in desk hours (8am-8pm = 84 h/week, shared between on-call people), 5 alerts/week x 30 min = 2.5 h active | 84 stated hours on call, 2.5-5 active | on-call stipend |
| Total concierge + support labour | | 25-40 productive hours | 1.5-2.5 people part-time |

Cost: part-time rate Rs 150-250/hour [U] x 25-40 h x 4.3 = **Rs 16,000-43,000/month** labour; emergency on-call stipend Rs 10,000-30,000/month [U]; telephony, STT/TTS and LLM Rs 50-150 per family/month [U, no spend limit set yet]. Total about **Rs 31,000-88,000 per 100 families per month, Rs 310-880 per family** [U].

Price implication [A]: if the price is only Rs 299-499/month (the PARENT_CARE_RESEARCH.md test range) the included concierge loses money for any active user; Emoha's cheapest published tier is far higher (from about Rs 7,999/month on its plans page, Rs 1,599 on an older page) [S]. Options: price at Rs 999-1,499 with a 2-request cap, or keep Rs 499 and make concierge pay-per-request above a cap, or price the human desk as a separate add-on. Break-even rule of thumb: cost per family stays under 50% of price.

### 3.5 Legal and regulatory flags (not legal advice; counsel needed)
- **Medical advice and tele-consult:** only a registered medical practitioner may do telemedicine; AI/ML platforms cannot counsel patients or prescribe; Schedule X and narcotics barred [S, Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020 via Mondaq/Khaitan]. Robin staff must not interpret symptoms or prescriptions; link out only.
- **Pharmacy / e-pharmacy:** selling or dispensing medicines needs a Drugs and Cosmetics licence; I could not confirm current e-pharmacy rules this session [U]. Safe design: order on an existing licensed e-pharmacy app from the child's account, never stock, hold or deliver.
- **Pay-on-behalf:** RBI regulates bill payment collection through BBPS operating units and payment aggregators with escrow [S, RBI Master Direction BBPS 2024-02-29 via search]; whether a concierge may pay a bill from its own funds for customers is unresolved [U]. Keep "user taps a UPI link" as the only path.
- **Authority and bank calls (112, 1930):** already gated in ROBIN_CONCIERGE_DESK.md.
- **Data:** DPDP Rules 2025 (notified 2025-11-14) for health, location and voice data; consent, retention and erasure; staff access logging [S, PARENT_CARE_RESEARCH.md]. Staff handling parents' prescriptions and addresses is the main new exposure vs an AI-only product.
- **Labour and liability:** part-timers or contractors handling distress calls; wellbeing support; insurance for errands that go wrong. Counsel to advise [U].
- **Marketing claims:** never "24/7", "we will call the police", "doctor" or "nurse" unless true and licensed.
- **Telecom:** TRAI/DLT rules for outbound calls to parents [U, from earlier research].

### 3.6 Ten questions for the founder
1. Price: is the concierge truly unlimited for the price, or capped (e.g. 2 requests per month)? Emoha caps at 6 h; every unlimited errand business died [S].
2. Which 8-10 request types in section 3.3 do you accept, and which do you strike?
3. Which cities for parents in Phase 1? Remote phone concierge works poorly outside metros [A].
4. Desk hours for lane 2: 8am-8pm, or longer with on-call? Who is primary and backup?
5. Who are the 2-3 part-timers, and are they employees or contractors (labour, liability, DPDP)?
6. Medicines: will you accept "order on an existing e-pharmacy from the child's account" and never touch dispensing?
7. Escorts and physical help: refer to a partner (Emoha, Samarth, Portea) or say plainly "not offered"?
8. Hulp and M (funded, AI plus humans) are moving in India: do you want to compete on elders and safety, or on breadth of errands?
9. Which metric ends the pilot successfully: concierge requests per family per week, minutes per request, or families who renew?
10. What is the maximum monthly loss per family you will accept during the pilot, and who pays for the legal review before real families join?
