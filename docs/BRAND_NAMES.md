# Brand name research (2026-10-11)

Requirement: .com AND .in both free, buyable at GoDaddy, fits a tech-savvy Gen-Z / millennial India audience, easy for a
salon receptionist to hear and say on a phone call.

Method: ~1,000 candidates (Hinglish words, compounds, coined names) checked against the registries' RDAP services
(`rdap.verisign.com` for .com, `rdap.nixiregistry.in` for .in): 404 = not registered. About 250 had both free; the list
below is the curated shortlist. Caveats: "not registered" is not the same as "cheap at GoDaddy" (premium or just-taken
names can still show); trademark NOT checked (use IP India public search: classes 9, 35, 38, 42); web search found no
conflicts for the shortlist, but only a weak search was done.

## Shortlist (all: .com and .in unregistered on 2026-10-11)
1. **Batiyaan** (batiyaan.com / batiyaan.in) - "chit-chat, talks": warm, desi, Gen-Z friendly. Also free: batiyan.com/.in
   (buy both to cover the spelling). No brand conflict found.
2. **Oyeping** (oyeping.com / .in) - "oye" (hey) + ping: playful, energetic. Alternatives free: oyering, oyedial, oyecall.
3. **Haanio** (haanio.com / .in) - "haan" (yes) + io: friendly; spelling/pronunciation less obvious. Also haanix.
4. **Hellowala** (hellowala.com / .in) - desi humour; may sound like a call centre.
5. **Callmisha** (callmisha.com / .in) - persona-led ("Call Misha"); depends on liking the name Misha.
6. **Kaamix** (kaamix.com / .in) - kaam (work) + mix; techy, slightly generic.
7. Baatwala / Baatpal, Vaanipal, Jaldiai (jaldiai.com / .in), Yaarnow.

## Avoid
- **Pucho / Puch**: "Puch AI" is a Bengaluru WhatsApp + voice AI assistant (around 1M users claimed): direct competitor, too close.
- **Bata** (shoe brand), **Friday** (generic, crowded, trademark and domain issues), single common words (.com taken).

## Round 2 (2026-10-11): the founder wants an AGENT CHARACTER name (a popular, human-style name), not a "wala" word
Finding: famous fictional assistant names (Jarvis, F.R.I.D.A.Y., Edith, Cortana, Moneypenny, Samantha) belong to other
companies' trademarks; using one as a commercial brand is risky (the current name "Friday" comes from the Iron Man AI).
Use a popular real first name with an agent feel, and brand it as "Agent <Name>" or "<Name> Agent".
.com + .in both unregistered (2026-10-11):
- **Zoya** (name of the spy in the Tiger films): agentzoya, zoyaagent, callzoya, getzoya, withzoya. Risk: Zoya jewellery (Titan) and a US nail brand exist; different classes, check IP India.
- **Misha**: agentmisha, mishaagent, callmisha, mishahq. **Kiara**: agentkiara, kiaraagent, callkiara.
- **Nyra** (modern, coined; fewest brand conflicts): agentnyra, nyraagent, callnyra, hellonyra.
- **Aarya**: agentaarya, aaryaagent, callaarya, getaarya. **Kavya**: agentkavya, kavyaai, kavyaagent.
Plain names without prefix (zoya.com, misha.com, ...) are all taken.

## Round 3 (2026-10-11): foreign (non-Indian) agent-character first names
Plain names + Aria/Ava/Nova/Eve/Luna/Stella/Iris/Ivy/Ada etc.: all taken as .com+.in in every pattern checked.
Both .com and .in free: **Ziva** (agentziva, callziva, meetziva, heyziva, withziva; NCIS agent), **Sloane** (agentsloane,
sloaneagent, callsloane, mysloane; spy name), Maeve (maeveagent, callmaeve; Westworld AI; hard to say), Lyra (calllyra),
Dani (agentdani, calldani, usedani), Romy (agentromy, romyagent), Ines, Mila, Thea, Willow, Isla. Nikita/Sasha are common in India.
Phone test: Ziva and Dani are the easiest for a salon receptionist to hear.

## Round 4 (2026-10-11): months / days / festivals / characters (anime, cartoon, movie)
Plain months, days and festival words are taken as .com+.in; patterns with agent/call/meet/with are free.
- Days/months (no character IP, best legal safety): **Thursday** (thursdayagent, callthursday, meetthursday, withthursday; nod to the spy novel
  "The Man Who Was Thursday"), **July** (agentjuly, calljuly), Tuesday (agenttuesday, tuesdayagent, calltuesday), Saturday, Marzo (March), August (callaugust).
- Wednesday (wednesdayagent, callwednesday) is the most famous (Addams Family) but that character is MGM/Netflix IP.
- Character-style first names: **Ziva**, **Sloane**, **Rei** (callrei, askrei, meetrei, withrei, tryrei; Evangelion), Hanna (agenthanna), Yor (callyor), Jem (calljem).
- Free but IP-heavy, do NOT use commercially: Naruto, Hinata, Mikasa, Asuka, Shinobu, Misa, Hermione, Gamora, Moana, Mulan, Leia, Wanda,
  Minnie, Tweety, Velma, Buttercup, Carmen (Sandiego), Villanelle.
- Festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Navratri, Onam, Pongal, Lohri, Baisakhi, Easter, Halloween) have agent/call/ask/meet patterns free, but
  tie the brand to one season and to religion; not recommended as the main brand.
