# Why Friday, when big companies build assistants (proposal, 14 Oct 2026)

Big players are strong at: general chat, global scale, devices, and software integrations.
They are weak at: acting in the messy offline Indian world, Hinglish phone calls, and earning trust
with small businesses. Friday should compete there, not in "ask me anything".

1. **Does the call for you, in Hinglish.** Most assistants answer; Friday phones the salon, PG,
   banquet or mover, compares and books. Indian small businesses run on phone calls, not APIs.
2. **Outcome, not chat.** One message when it is done, with a result and proof (transcript summary).
3. **Never exceeds your limits.** Code-level checks: your budget, your time, never pays, never
   OTP/PIN. Big firms cannot easily promise this for an open-ended agent.
4. **Your data is used only for you.** Never shared across users; export and one-step delete.
   The only shared learning is how businesses talk, anonymised.
5. **Learns how Indian businesses talk.** Our listening model improves from real call data
   (a data moat: labelled Hinglish vendor replies). Spoken words stay scripted and approved.
6. **Scripted, not improvised voice.** Predictable, no hallucinated prices, auditable.
7. **Negotiates inside your range.** Pushes for a better price, never above your maximum.
8. **Persistent follow-through.** Retries, busy-slot callbacks, reminders, re-confirm the day before.
9. **Local trust.** Vendor-side info page, opt-out list, call-back number, Truecaller-style trust.
10. **Phase 2: chief of staff** from Gmail/calendar, for that user only, with the same limits.

## Suggested positioning (to decide)
"Friday makes the calls you hate making." Pick 3 of the above to lead with: #1, #3, #4.

## Hard to copy vs easy to copy
- Hard: the labelled Hinglish business-conversation data, the vendor trust relationships, the
  safety-by-code limits.
- Easy: the voice, the WhatsApp channel, general reasoning. Do not build the brand on these.

## Moat and fundability: what Instinct and Ghost have, and what Friday should add (14 Oct 2026)

Facts from docs/COMPETITORS.md (several figures are press or competitor blogs, unverified):
Instinct = viral growth (100k+ users), a young founder from Sierra, $1B at $10B (Sep 2026), Concierge
calls, integrations (Google, Stripe, Shopify), agent-to-agent with business bots, no India/Hindi.
Ghost = privacy-first, always-on agents, trust story.
Investors fund: a big market, fast growth, something hard to copy, and a team that can ship.

### What to add (ranked)
1. **A data moat that compounds.** Every call teaches how Indian businesses talk (labelled Hinglish
   vendor replies). Track it as a number: labelled sentences, intents covered, accuracy. This is the
   asset investors can see grow.
2. **India-first on purpose.** Hinglish, UPI, WhatsApp, Indian small businesses, DPDP-ready. No US
   rival has launched here (as of 12 Oct). The window is real but short.
3. **Vendor-side network.** Businesses that list a WhatsApp agent or call-back number get "Friday
   verified" (info page, opt-out). Two-sided networks are hard to copy. Later: agent-to-agent with
   vendors' own bots, calling only as the fallback.
4. **Proof and trust as product.** Outcome page with a transcript summary, a public "what Friday never
   does", code-level spending and time limits, one-step delete and export. This is what Ghost sells.
5. **Retention loop.** Re-confirm the day before, repeat bookings ("haircut every 5 weeks"), saved
   favourite vendors, family profiles (per user only). Calls are rare; follow-ups bring users back.
6. **Revenue model early.** Options to decide: free first requests then a monthly plan, or a small
   fee per booking, or a vendor-paid lead fee (never ranked by who pays: that breaks trust).
7. **Metrics investors ask for.** Requests completed, success rate, cost per call, repeat rate,
   referrals, vendor opt-outs. Start logging them now.
8. **Distribution.** Invite waves (10, 30, 50), referral by outcome page link, creators, resident
   welfare groups, offices.
9. **Phase 2 wedge: customer-care and hold fighting** (ISP, bank, cable). Strong demand, and Instinct
   and Muse already target it, so decide launch versus fast follow.

### Honest limits
- Nobody can promise funding or survival. Instinct's valuation is US-market and hype-driven.
- Friday's real risk: a big company launches in India with Hindi. The defence is speed, local data,
  vendor trust, and being the safest option to hand a phone call to.
- We need real usage numbers before any pitch; claims without data hurt more than they help.

### Founder decisions needed
Which 3 of the above lead the story; the revenue model; whether customer-care calls are a launch
feature; the vendor "verified" programme.
